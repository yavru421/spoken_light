📋 Sunday Sound Booth Volunteer Cheat Sheet
Step 1: Check Input & VU Bounce
Confirm Soundboard Input Device is selected in the dropdown below. When the pastor speaks into the lapel mic, verify the green VU meter is bouncing.
Step 2: Start Service
Click ▶ START LIVE CAPTIONS. Subtitles will immediately appear in the teleprompter box below and broadcast to the OBS livestream overlay.
Step 3: Spacebar for Worship Songs (Mute)
Tap Spacebar when congregation singing begins to mute captions so lyrics aren't garbled. Tap Spacebar again when the pastor begins preaching.
Step 4: Stop Service & Safety Backup
Click ⏹ STOP LIVE CAPTIONS at sermon conclusion. Click 💾 Save Transcript (.txt) at any time for an instant local text file backup. (If forgotten, the 15-min inactivity timer auto-finalizes and archives to Cloud D1).
