Organization & Purpose

Spoken Light is a real-time AI live-captioning and low-latency audio transport bridge custom-engineered for Calvary Baptist Church (Wisconsin Rapids, WI). It captures stream audio input and dispatches low-latency 16kHz PCM chunks to a Cloudflare Workers AI Whisper edge pipeline for live lower-third OBS overlay rendering.
