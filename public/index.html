Direct hardware ingest, instant Bible chapter detection, and zero-liability edge architecture.

📺

Native OBS Transparent Lower-Third

A 100% transparent browser source link (/overlay?room=calvary). Drops cleanly over your camera feed in OBS Studio with high-contrast ProPresenter-style typography and scripture banners.

📖

Scripture Anchor Auto-Slicing

Listens in real-time. When the pastor speaks "Romans 8:28" or "John 3:16", Spoken Light automatically anchors the timeline, slices a new chapter, and highlights the verse.

⌨️

Single Spacebar Worship Mute

Hit the soundboard keyboard spacebar during worship music to instantly mute subtitles so singing isn’t garbled. Hit it again when the sermon begins. Zero friction.

🧠

Post-Sermon Pastoral Notes

The moment the service ends, Spoken Light generates an executive summary, 3 bulleted theological takeaways, and chapter sub-summaries for your pastor's newsletter or archive.

🛡️

15-Min Inactivity Auto-Finalizer

If volunteers walk away after the postlude and leave the soundboard running, our Durable Object alarm automatically finalizes the record and seals the archive without human intervention.

💾

Dual-Channel Offline Safety

Transcripts save directly to Cloudflare D1 while simultaneously keeping a local .txt backup in soundboard memory so you never lose a message even if the sanctuary router hiccups.
